# Product Sop

Updated 2026-11-05. Local implementation only. No production writes, remote push or deployment. Hosted Railway staging remains stopped. Real providers and physical hardware require separate acceptance.

Product specifications can be authored in Markdown/custom sections and associated with product IDs. Required step rules and evidence/scan requirements are persisted and enforced at QC. Product-specific fabric composition, GSM, embroidery, seam/stitching, label location and size tolerances are administrator content; no manufacturing values are seeded as truth. The section editor and configurable measurement rules capture approved specifications; QC enforces required values and numeric bounds.
